If this is urgent, please don't wait

This form is not monitored continuously. For an emergency, call 000. For an urgent, non-emergency risk of serious harm, call the NDIS Quality and Safeguards Commission on 1800 035 544.

What happens next

We'll take time to properly understand what you've told us, and look into the matter fairly. If you've left contact details and told us we may use them, we'll be in touch — including if we need more information.

We aim to acknowledge submissions as soon as we can. Some matters are more complex and take a little longer to look into; we'll keep you informed where we're able to.
