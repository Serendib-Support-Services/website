Make a referral

Refer to support that's reliable, respectful and ready to help

Whether you're a participant, a family member or carer, a support coordinator or a plan manager, making a referral to Serendib Support Services is simple. Tell us what's needed and we'll take it from there — calmly and clearly.

Go to referral form Prefer to talk first?
Who can refer

Anyone in the participant's circle of support

You don't need to be the participant to start a referral. We welcome referrals from everyone involved in a person's support.

Participants

Refer yourself directly — we'll guide you gently through what happens next.

Families & carers

Refer a loved one you support — with their knowledge and consent.

Support coordinators

Refer participants on your caseload and we'll keep you updated throughout.

Plan managers & nominees

Connect participants with dependable support and clear documentation.

Before you start

What information helps

Don't worry if you don't have everything — share what you can and we'll follow up on the rest. These details just help us understand the support needed.

  • The participant's name and a contact person
  • Suburb and the best way to get in touch
  • How the plan is managed (self-managed or plan-managed)
  • The type of support being looked for
  • Preferred days and times, if known
  • Any notes or documents that give helpful context
What happens next

Your referral pathway

1

We receive your referral

You'll get a friendly acknowledgement that we've received the details.

2

We get in touch

We reach out to talk through goals, needs and any questions you have.

3

We plan the support

Together we agree on supports and put a clear service agreement in place.

Support begins

You're matched with suitable workers and we check in to keep things on track.
