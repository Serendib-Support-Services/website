What we offer

Everyday supports, clearly explained

Mix and match the supports you need. Everything below can be tailored to your plan, your routine and the way you like things done.

Daily Living Support

Personalised NDIS daily living support in Melbourne — help with everyday routines, getting ready for the day, personal organisation and the regular tasks that keep home life running smoothly.

Community Access

Confident NDIS community access support in Melbourne — visiting places you enjoy, joining activities and staying connected to your local community.

Transport Assistance

Reliable NDIS transport assistance in Melbourne — getting to appointments, activities and errands, with a familiar worker alongside you to help the journey go smoothly.

Appointment Support

Help to prepare for, get to and follow up on appointments — so important dates are organised and nothing slips through the cracks.

Shopping & Errands

Support with grocery shopping, picking up essentials and everyday errands — done with you or for you, however you prefer.

Meal Preparation

A hand with planning and preparing meals you enjoy — supporting healthy routines and a bit more independence in the kitchen.

Household Tasks

NDIS household tasks support in Melbourne — help with light tidying, laundry and keeping things organised, so your home stays comfortable and manageable.

Life Skills Development

Build everyday skills step by step — from routines and organisation to growing confidence and independence at a pace that suits you.

Social Participation

Support to connect with others and take part in social activities, groups and interests — building friendships and a sense of belonging.

  • Safety documentation
  • Ongoing support planning & reviews
