Why choose Serendib

Support that feels personal, organised and genuinely reliable

We believe good support starts with listening. We take time to understand your routines, your preferences and your goals — then match you with workers who turn up, follow through and treat you with dignity.

  • Consistency you can count on — familiar workers and dependable scheduling, so support feels steady.
  • Built around your goals — we document what matters to you and keep your supports pointed in that direction.
  • Respect, choice and control — you decide how support is delivered, and we listen and adapt.
  • Easy to work with — clear communication, tidy documentation and prompt responses for everyone involved.
  • Culturally aware matching — where possible, we match you with a worker who shares your language or cultural background, if that helps you feel more at home.

How support starts

Getting started is simple — three calm steps

No confusing forms or pressure. We make the first steps clear, so you always know what happens next.

1

Get in touch

Make a referral or request a call back. Tell us a little about the participant and the support you're looking for — there's no obligation.

2

Plan together

We talk through goals, routines and preferences, then agree on supports and put a clear service agreement in place.

3

Begin your support

You're matched with workers who suit you, support begins, and we check in regularly to keep things on track.
